# Maizz privacy notice (DRAFT for your lawyer)

This is plain-language draft text, not legal advice. Maizz must be registered with Ghana's Data Protection Commission before real givers' details are kept. The retention periods marked [lawyer] need a decision from your lawyer.

## Who we are
Maizz is a giving service for churches in Ghana, run by [company name, as on the registration] ("Maizz", "we"). Contact: [privacy email address]. [Registration number with the Data Protection Commission, once you have it.]

## What we collect, and why
When you give through a church's Maizz page:
- **Your name** (unless you choose to give anonymously). So your church can thank you and keep its records.
- **Your mobile money number.** To ask your phone to approve the gift. It is passed to our payment partner to take the payment. We never see your PIN.
- **Your email** (only if you give one). To send you a receipt.
- **The gift:** the amount, the date, the type of gift (for example tithe or offering) and which church it went to.
- **A scrambled fingerprint of your device's internet address and of your phone number**, kept for a short time to stop abuse and repeated requests. It cannot be turned back into the number or address, and it is deleted within two days.

If you choose to give anonymously, we keep the gift but not your name, number or email.

## What we do not collect
Card numbers and mobile money PINs. Cards are entered on our payment partner's page, and PINs on your own phone.

## Who sees it
- **Your church** sees the gift (amount, date, type) and, unless you gave anonymously, your name. Church staff do not see your phone number.
- **Our payment partner** handles the payment and sees what it needs to take it.
- **Maizz** can see gift records to run the service and settle disputes.
- If you reach Maizz from your church's Church Manager app and give with your name, the gift is tagged with a private member code from that app so your church's app can show you your own giving. The code has no name, number or email in it, and anonymous gifts are never tagged.
- We do not sell your details or use them for advertising.

## How long we keep it
- A giver whose gift never completed: name, number and email are removed after 30 days.
- A giver whose gift completed: [lawyer: how long records of completed gifts must be kept, for example for accounting and tax, and whether names can be removed earlier while the amounts stay in the books].
- Church applications that were declined: contact details removed after 90 days.
- Short-lived abuse counters: deleted within two days.

## Your rights
You can ask to see what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it removed (we remove personal details; the amounts stay in the books because the law requires records of money). Write to [privacy email]. We will answer within [lawyer: period]. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission.
