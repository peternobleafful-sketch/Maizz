Use the attached design.md as the design system for everything below. Follow it exactly. The attached images are inspiration for mood only.

Build a complete, clickable design for Maizz, a giving platform for churches in Ghana. Design phone screens first (390px wide), then a desktop version of each. Use real sample content: Grace Chapel Accra, amounts in GH₵, Ghanaian names, MTN / Vodafone / AirtelTigo mobile money.

Group the work in this order and keep every screen consistent.

1. Giver giving page (the most important)
 a. Amount-first page: blue hero with white logo, church name, huge amount input with GH₵ prefix, quick amounts 20/50/100/200, then a white sheet with gift type chips (Tithe, Offering, Thanksgiving, Seed, Building fund, Missions, Other), network chips, phone number, "Give anonymously" checkbox, name field, receipt summary (Gift, Fees, Total, "{Church} receives the full GH₵X"), full-width "Give GH₵X" button, and the line "You approve the payment on your phone. Maizz never sees your PIN."
 b. States: empty, filled, field errors, waiting ("Check your phone", pulsing ring), enter one-time code, confirming, paid (lime circle, self-drawing tick, "Thank you", reference), failed (what to do next), church not found, church paused, test-mode pill version.
 c. Desktop: phone-width column centred on the blue field.

2. Receipts
 Email receipt and on-screen receipt: church, date, gift type, gift, fees, total, reference. No payment provider name.

3. Church sign-up and setup
 Short sign-up form, "pending review" screen, add payout account (mobile money or bank, only last 4 digits shown afterwards), confirm owner, activate, "Your giving page is live" with link, QR code and a printable-looking share card.

4. Church dashboard (sign-in with two-step code required)
 Sign-in, two-step code, overview (given this week and month, number of gifts, by gift type, simple chart), gifts list with filters (date, type, anonymous shown as "Anonymous"), gift detail, payouts list (amount, date, status), export, team members with roles (owner, finance, viewer), settings. Empty states for a new church with no gifts yet. Mobile and desktop.

5. Church Manager link
 A "My giving" screen inside the Church Manager app for a member: their own giving history, yearly total, a "Give now" button that opens the web checkout page (no in-app pay button), and a year-end statement view.

6. Maizz owner tools (internal)
 White, calm, utility look: church list with status tags, church detail, daily check results with lime PASS and dashed-blue FIX tags, refund flow with confirmation, audit log table.

7. Marketing home page
 Simple "coming soon" hero now, plus a fuller version: what Maizz is, how it works in three steps, fees explained plainly (givers cover fees, church gets the full gift), security promises, contact. Blue hero, white logo, soft gradient.

8. System pieces
 Buttons, inputs, chips, tags, tables, toasts, empty states, loading skeletons, error pages (404, 500, offline), app icon and favicon from the Maizz mark.

Deliver as a design canvas with labelled artboards per group, phone and desktop side by side. Then list anything in the brief you changed or could not do.
